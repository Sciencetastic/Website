The ritual

How it works

  1. I

    Wake the board

    Power on the board. Your phone connects to it.

  2. II

    Ask your question

    Hold the Speak button and ask the spirits your question out loud.

  3. III

    Lay a card

    Place one of the 78 RFID tarot cards on the board.

  4. IV

    The spirit answers

    A spirit appears in the crystal ball and interprets your card. It may ask you a clarifying question — answer it, or interrupt whenever you like.

Under the board

The tech, in plain English

  • Your phone is the brain. It runs the game and the spirits.
  • The board reads the cards. The deck has 78 tarot cards, each with two RFID chips inside, so the board knows which card you placed.
  • The spirit is an illusion. The image is projected into the crystal ball using Pepper's Ghost, a classic stage trick with angled glass.
  • The AI runs on your phone. The spirits' readings are generated on the device itself.
