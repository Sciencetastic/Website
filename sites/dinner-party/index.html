Design at a glance

  • Engine: Visionaire Studio — chosen to make a Switch version easier.
  • Genre: point-and-click survival horror adventure in the tradition of the classic MacVenture games. [TBD — confirm still current]
  • Look & sound: Genesis-era style (320×240, 61 colours, Genesis sound chips). [TBD — confirm 2020 pitch specs are still current]
  • Play: several playable guests who see objects differently, multiple solutions and no dead ends, and endings that change with who survives. [TBD — confirm still current]

FAQ

What's the difference between the beta and the demo?

The beta is an early test build for a smaller group of players, to find bugs and give feedback before things are final. The demo is a free, public slice of the game that anyone can play to try it out.

When does the beta start?

Soon — the exact date is to be announced. Sign up below and you'll be told first.
