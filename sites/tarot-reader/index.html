The ritual

How it works

  1. I

    Lay the cards

    Place RFID-equipped tarot cards into the slots on the board. The board knows which card is where.

  2. II

    Light the crystal ball

    Your phone's screen projects into the crystal ball using the Pepper's Ghost illusion — a classic stage trick with angled glass.

  3. III

    A spirit appears

    A 3D spirit avatar materialises inside the ball, ready to talk with you.

  4. IV

    Hear your reading

    The spirit gives your fortune reading in its own voice and personality.

Under the board

The tech, in plain English

  • The AI runs on your phone. Readings come from a lightweight language model that runs locally, on the device — it's designed for any phone with 4 GB of RAM or more.
  • Each spirit is its own character. When you switch spirits, the app loads a different add-on for the model (a “LoRA”, which tunes its personality) and a different voice.
  • The cards are real cards. Each tarot card carries an RFID tag, so the board can tell which cards you've laid down.
  • The ghost is an illusion. Pepper's Ghost reflects your phone screen so the spirit seems to float inside the ball.

Status & honest blocker

It works. After three years of development, the prototype has proven the idea.

The hard part: manufacturing the cards. There isn't yet a reliable way to assemble the RFID tarot cards at scale.

Not for sale yet. No price, no ship date.

Made for Halloween parties

Gather everyone around the board, dim the lights, and take turns with the spirits. It's a party gimmick built for group readings and good screams.

Readings are for entertainment only — no claim of real fortune-telling.
